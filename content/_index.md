<h1 id="top">Swaw</h1>

Swaw is the public-facing home of an owner-operated company.
It is not meant to flatten every update into one long feed.
The home page does three jobs first: define the brand, point to the products, and route readers into the ongoing signals.

[Go to Products](/products/)
[Go to Signals](/d/)
[Read About Swaw](/about/)

<h2 id="products">Products</h2>

Products is where Swaw collects outputs that are ready to be used, bought, deployed, or maintained over time.
The home page only frames that direction; the full catalog stays on the dedicated products page.

[Browse Products](/products/)

<h2 id="signals">Signals</h2>

Signals is where Swaw publishes notes, observations, methods, and directional judgment.
It works as the brand's ongoing voice, rather than as an appendix to the homepage.

[Enter Signals](/d/)

<h2 id="about">About</h2>

The home page keeps only the short identity layer.
The longer explanation of the brand, operating names, and how this site is organized belongs on the dedicated about page.

[Visit /about](/about/)
